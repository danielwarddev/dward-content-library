For a game with a setting kind of like Fallout and/or EYE:
- Insect Master
	- Play on: tamer, druid, animal whisperer, summoner
	- Abilities like: summon diff bugs for diff effects,  temp armor increases, temp debuffs, enemies can spend time killing bugs to weaken the master, create a swarm, combine the swarm into one powerful legion monster, access the hive, see what their bugs see, control a bug directly to use as a drone
	- Rather than full control, Insect Masters have a symbiotic relationship with insects they consider close, called "symbiotes." Insect Masters prefer to let their minions do the dirty work, applying debuffs and damage over time while staying out of harms way themselves. In return, the Insect Master allows the symbiotes to nourish themselves from their own body, sacrificing health, rust, and other resources to activate abilities. However, an Insect Master is little without their insects.
- Rust Mage
	- Play on: mages
	- Abilities like: Gaara's sand stuff, CC by grabbing, big damage but high cost, more rust puddles = more damage, aoe, dot, high single target damage
	- Although all classes use the so-called rust puddles scattered throughout the land after the great destruction, the Rust Mage is the only one to do so as its exclusive source of energy. Rust Mages treat the puddles as an extension of their own body and turn them into weapons, which works quite well for how ubiquitous they are. The more rust there is, the more powerful a Rust Mage is.
- Cyber Knight
	- Play on: knight/warrior
	- Abilities like: Weapon abilities, increase defense/offense, unique playstyles from different cyber options that can be changed in towns
	- Cyber Knights are the typical knight one might expect to see in the realm - wearing strong armor, wielding large martial weapons, and regularly harnessing the cyberworld to tap into its power and imbue their abilities with it. Cyber Knights aren't the fastest, but they bring the law with them, and the law always arrives. What they lack in speed they make up for in raw power and defense, as well as their cyber ability choice.
- War Merchant
	- Play on: ?
	- Abilities like: 
	- A heavy hand or clever spell can both be effective methods of combat, but so can the careful flick of a tongue. War Merchants, as their name implies, are charismatic individuals who rather find themselves in a position of resources by way of influencing others to do their bidding for them. While a War Merchant need not literally be a seller of goods, they often use their abilities for financial gain, for this leads to all other gain. War Merchants earn their moniker moreso by their aptitude for power than their actual business.

- Astral Zealout
	- Play on: divine, god class, blessings
	- Playstyle changes as you go through rust puddles

"Rust puddles" are brown splotches of limited? resource scattered throughout levels and boss rooms. All classes use them in some way for resources. At the same time, bosses and enemies will use these, as well, to do things like detonate them and cast "spells" from, so they are are simultaneously to be sometimes avoided.
