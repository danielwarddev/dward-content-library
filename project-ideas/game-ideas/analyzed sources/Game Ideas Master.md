### 4X Space Game

**Genre:** 4X Strategy / RPG Hybrid  
**Scope:** Full game

#### Summary

A 4X-like space game where the player starts as a low-status citizen and works their way up the social ladder to eventually rule an empire, managing increasingly complex resources along the way.

#### Core Mechanics

-   Start as a poor citizen (farmer, office employee, homeless, etc.)
-   Progress through social ranks: citizen → town leader → government worker → sector leader → committee member → vice president → president
-   Resource management scales with status: early game is food/energy/money for survival; late game is armies/cities/population/food distribution/mining
-   Objective-based progression with rewards (contracts, bounties)
-   Procedurally generated worlds with multiple systems, planets, races, cultures, ecosystems, technologies, cities, economies, governments, and armies
-   Interaction with alien animals and sentient civilizations
-   Goal: rule over or form alliances with other life; defeat resistors through battle, politics, or resources

#### The 4X Elements

-   **Explore:** (Design notes incomplete in source)
-   **Expand:** (Design notes incomplete in source)
-   **Exploit:** (Design notes incomplete in source)
-   **Exterminate:** Conquer other life through various means, not necessarily violent

#### Additional Notes

-   Multiplayer is optional, not critical
-   The game should always have additional goals even at the highest level of power

### MMO Concept

**Genre:** MMORPG  
**Scope:** Full game (large)

#### Summary

A skill-based MMO with flexible character building, where players can level any stat/talent they want, creating highly customizable builds without rigid class restrictions.

#### Attributes

-   **STR:** Physical abilities more powerful (melee and ranged)
-   **WIS:** Magical abilities more powerful (melee and ranged)
-   **INT:** Increases MP
-   **VIT:** Increases HP
-   **HEL:** Healing abilities more powerful

_Note: Consider removing attributes entirely and converting to talents for more flexibility._

#### Talents System

Talents are skills like Sword, Axe, Hammer, Scythe, Dagger, Rapiers, Bow, Greatbow, Staff, etc.

**Key Design Points:**

-   Level up talents through use
-   Skill points are redistributable in safe havens (towns)
-   Exponential cost curve: low levels cheap, high levels expensive
-   XP gained based on monster level (not just usage count) to help late-game talent switching
-   Leveling talents unlocks skills for that talent
-   Some talents may require prerequisites (like FFT job requirements)

**Possible Talents:**

-   Weapons: Sword, Greatsword, Dagger, Whip, Axe, Hammer, Mace, Rapier, Bow, Greatbow, Crossbow, Shuriken, Staff
-   Magic: Elemental Magic, Healing Magic, Green Magic (buffs/debuffs), Time Magic, Summoning
-   Other: Evade, Agility, Courage, Guard, Beastmastery, Dextrous (enables dual-wielding), Alchemy/Herblore
-   Transformation talents: Lichhood, Vampirism, Wings (unlocked via quests/requirements, may have form-specific limitations)

#### Skills

-   Acquired through talents, monsters, achievements, attributes, level, weapon use, or combinations
-   Can have passive skills at high talent levels
-   Fun unlocks: kill certain monsters enough → gain cosmetic/joke skills

#### Weapons

**Customization Options:**

1. **Mods** (Guild Wars style): Attach mods to weapons for bonuses (e.g., Time Magic +x%)
2. **Parts** (piece-by-piece): Weapons drop as parts (hilt, jewel, blade) that combine; appearance changes based on parts

**Universal Parts Concept:**

-   Blades: work on swords, daggers, rapiers, scythes, arrows
-   Wood: hilts, staves, bows, arrow shafts
-   String: bows

**Weapon Considerations:**

-   Attack speed and range should factor into balance
-   Certain weapons may complement certain talents
-   Weight affects run speed (armor only? all equipment? inventory?)
-   Weapons could have dual effects: one for physical, one for magical

#### Combat & Balance Notes

-   Avoid skill/buff overload (WAR had 30 buffs at once - confusing)
-   Encapsulate effects like Guild Wars did (conditions, hexes, enchantments)
-   Important bonuses at talent intervals (e.g., 50 sword = +4 str)
-   Consider PvP focus: 1v1, 2v2, team-based?
-   Team PvP is more strategic; 1v1 relies more on current skills

#### Elements

-   Player attuned to one element at a time
-   Each element has certain effects
-   Risk: may reduce viable builds if not balanced

#### Other Ideas

-   Take notes from Runescape quests for immersive, meaningful storylines
-   UI updates can reinvigorate players
-   Social hangout spots need rewards or players leave
-   Randomly-generated wide-area PvP maps (partial random: static bases, random terrain)
-   Titles for talent milestones (newbie → beginner → apprentice → etc.)
-   Aesthetic-only drops if focusing on player skill over grinding
-   Birthday/holiday events
-   Long-term goals: plants, pets, houses
-   Effect-based skill creation (like Morrowind spell crafting)

#### Sample Skills

-   **Prepared Stance:** Enter a stance enabling Preparation moves; -x armor while active; removed by moving or CC
-   **Drink from the Pool:** Reduce all skill costs to 0, use Energy from the Pool instead; drains when damaged
-   **Aura of Spikes:** All skills cost more mana but inflict Cripple
-   **Empowerment of Flame:** Every 4th attack imbues weapon with bonus damage and Blue Fire DoT

### Post-Apocalyptic RPG (Fallout/EYE-like)

**Genre:** Action RPG  
**Scope:** Full game

#### Summary

A post-apocalyptic game with Fallout/EYE vibes featuring unique classes that all interact with "rust puddles" - a shared resource scattered throughout levels that both players and enemies use.

#### Classes

**Insect Master**

-   Playstyle: Tamer, druid, summoner
-   Has symbiotic relationship with insects called "symbiotes"
-   Abilities: summon different bugs for different effects, temp armor increases, temp debuffs, create swarms, combine swarm into legion monster, access the hive, see through bugs, control bug as drone
-   Sacrifices health, rust, and other resources to activate abilities
-   Weak without their insects

**Rust Mage**

-   Playstyle: Classic mage
-   Uses rust puddles as exclusive energy source
-   Abilities: Gaara-like sand manipulation, CC by grabbing, high damage but high cost, more rust = more damage, AoE, DoT, high single target
-   The more rust puddles nearby, the more powerful

**Cyber Knight**

-   Playstyle: Knight/warrior
-   Abilities: Weapon abilities, defense/offense buffs, unique playstyles from different cyber options (changeable in towns)
-   Wears strong armor, wields large martial weapons, harnesses cyberworld power
-   Slow but powerful with high defense

**War Merchant**

-   Playstyle: Charismatic manipulator
-   Uses influence to make others fight for them
-   Often uses abilities for financial gain
-   Named for aptitude for power rather than actual business

**Grease Nurse**

-   Playstyle: Healer, engineer/turrets
-   (Further details not specified)

**Astral Zealot**

-   Playstyle: Divine, god class, blessings
-   Playstyle changes as you go through rust puddles
-   (Further details not specified)

#### Rust Puddles

-   Brown splotches of limited(?) resource scattered throughout levels and boss rooms
-   All classes use them for resources in different ways
-   Enemies also use them: detonate, cast "spells" from them
-   Creates dynamic where puddles are sometimes to be sought, sometimes avoided

### Creature Collector Game (Pokemon-like)

**Genre:** Creature Collector RPG  
**Scope:** Full game

#### Summary

A Pokemon-like creature collector with improved shiny hunting, better UI for status effects, and exploration rewards.

#### Design Notes

-   **Shiny Hunting:** Use Coromon's approach - two tiers of shinies:
    -   Mid-tier: ~2% chance (~10% with item) - still exciting but achievable
    -   Rare tier: Normal shiny rates - keeps the ultra-rare feeling special
-   **Weather/Ability Effects:** Don't show repeated pop-ups; display on UI instead
-   **Economy:** Coromon has better balance of money/item prices/difficulty
-   **Exploration Rewards:** Exploring houses has benefits (e.g., finding plant pots permanently adds them to fruit drone for remote planting/harvesting)

#### Monster Checklist (Development)

-   Front/back sprites + animations
-   Cry
-   Shiny palettes
-   Moveset
-   Types, abilities, stats, move pool, catch rate, breeding info

### Color Puzzle Game

**Genre:** Puzzle  
**Scope:** Full game (mobile-friendly)

#### Summary

A color-matching puzzle game with 5 worlds of 8 levels each, progressively introducing mechanics like combos, multiple colors, bad colors, text misdirection, and power-ups.

#### Structure

-   5 worlds, 8 levels each
-   Bonus rounds after levels 4 and 8
-   Stars required to unlock worlds
-   Something special for completing all 3 stars on everything (new world unlock?)
-   Free mode available

#### Mechanics Progression

1. **Basic:** Click correct colors, time requirements
2. **Points:** Score requirements added
3. **Speed:** Progressively faster
4. **Combos:** Combo requirements
5. **Multiple Colors:** 2-3 colors to track
6. **Bad Colors:** Avoid certain colors
7. **Text Misdirection:** Text displays which color but text/color don't always match
8. **Power-ups:** Help achieve requirements
9. **Different Grids:** Various grid shapes
10. **Invisible Grid:** (Secret world?)

#### World 1 Breakdown

1. Intro, slow + low points
2. Faster + more points
3. Intro to time req
4. Faster + less time
5. Intro to combos
6. Intro to powerups
7. Faster powerups (standard speed from here on)
8. Everything together: points, combo, powerups, speed ramp

#### World 2 Breakdown

1. 2 colors, avg speed
2. 3 colors, avg speed
3. 3 colors, speeds up, combo req
4. Bad colors intro: 2 good + 1 bad
5. 1 good + 1 bad, speeds up, combo req
6. 2 good + 1 bad, speeds up, combo req
7. Text misdirection: rotating good + 1 bad
8. Text misdirection + combo + time req

### Physics-Based Platformer

**Genre:** Platformer / Action  
**Scope:** Medium

#### Summary

A platformer game (think Mega Man Zero style) where your attacks are physics-based rather than using static attack animations. You wield a bladed whip weapon controlled by a physics system.

#### Core Mechanics

-   Send your weapon out in front and pull it back using physics
-   Blade collisions with enemies deal damage (possibly based on speed)
-   Enemies can be cut/sliced in specific spots like Mega Man Zero (e.g., robot gets sliced in half vertically or horizontally)

#### Design Questions

### RPG Battle System (Tiered Turns)

**Scope:** Reusable system for turn-based RPGs

#### Core Concept

You have a Turn 1 set of skills, Turn 2 set, Turn 3 set, etc. Each set can take any spell, but depending on which set, will access a more powerful tier of it. A tier can also cast any lower tier version of the spell.

#### Example: Healer Class

**Set Layout:**

-   Skill 1: Heal/Support
-   Skill 2: Buff
-   Skill 3: Damage/Condition

**Heal Skill Tiers:**

-   T1: Heal 1 target a low/moderate amount
-   T2: Heal all targets a low amount
-   T3: Heal all targets a high amount
-   T4: Fully heal a target
-   T5: Fully heal all targets

So you can either cure conditions or heal on Turn 1, but not both - but you can do the other on Turn 2.

**Note:** Would probably work best with a battle system that fully heals after each battle.
