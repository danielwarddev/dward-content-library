Stats
1. POW – Power. How strong the dog is.
2. SPD – Speed. Both how fast and how agile the dog is.
3. INT – Intelligence. How smart the dog is.
4. BARK – Bark. The effectiveness of the dog’s bark.
5. SIZE – Size. How big the dog is.
6. LEAD – Leadership. The leadership skills of the dog to become the alpha of the pack.
7. TAIL - Total Attitude Indicator Length. Tails affect everything from cuteness, to balance, to communication, so the longer, the better! This affects a bit of everything.

The Pack
The player’s party is referred to as their “pack.” A pack consists of 4 party members of the player’s currently available companions. The pack can only be changed when in a town. The party leader is referred to as the “alpha” and will be displayed on the overworld as the character sprite.


Companions
A companion is a dog currently with the player, whether in the pack or not. Companions can be found in two different ways.
