## Conference Profile

- **Focus:** AI, machine learning, web frameworks, testing, design, product management, and leadership — rooted in culture, diversity, and community
- **Tagline:** "The South's flagship tech & AI conference for developers and modern builders"
- **Vibe:** Festival-style tech conference merging code, culture, and community. Very inclusive, culturally relevant. Includes a music concert (RenderFEST), food truck park, immersive demos, and networking events across Atlanta. NOT a typical corporate conference — more like a tech festival celebration.
- **Founded:** 2019 by Justin E. Samuels. Originally a React-focused conference, now a full-scale tech experience.
- **Size/Stature:** One of the largest tech conferences in the United States. Sponsors/exhibitors include Netflix, GitHub, Microsoft, Google, AWS, Shopify, Uber, Epic Games, Capital One, Zillow, and many more.

## Target Audience

Per the website, the conference targets:

- A.I. & M.L. Engineers
- Software Developers
- Architects
- Engineering & Product Leaders
- UI/UX Designers

Sessions are geared toward attendees with **at least 3 years of experience**.

## Topics They're Looking For

Based on the CFP description and 2025 programming:

### High-Interest Topics

- **AI/ML:** Agents, agentic RAG, vibe coding, practical AI applications, AI monitoring, responsible AI, AI energy crisis, AI for business
- **Web Technologies:** Modern frameworks, JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js (especially with OpenJS Summit partnership)
- **Developer Productivity:** AI-assisted coding (GitHub Copilot, MCP), testing, DevOps
- **Product & Leadership:** Scaling culture, remote/hybrid work, engineering leadership, career growth
- **Design:** UI/UX in AI era, accessible design, product design

### What They DON'T Want

- Product pitches or vendor demos (these get rejected unread)
- Content that's just README-level documentation
- Easily searchable information — they want unique insights
