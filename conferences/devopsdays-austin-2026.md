# DevOpsDays Austin 2026

## Conference Details

- **Event:** DevOpsDays Austin 2026
- **Dates:** May 5-6, 2026 (Tuesday-Wednesday)
- **Location:** Austin, TX (venue TBD)
- **Website:** https://devopsdays.org/austin
- **CFP Link:** https://talks.devopsdays.org/devopsdays-austin-2026/cfp
- **CFP Deadline:** February 26, 2026 ⚠️ **URGENT — ~3 weeks away!**

## Speaker Benefits

- ❌ No travel budget
- ❌ No hotel/sponsorship for speakers
- ✅ Conference admission (assumed)

## Conference Profile

- **Focus:** DevOps culture, process, and technology — with 2026 theme **"Value All The Way Down"** (delivering value to customers)
- **Size/vibe:** Community-driven, 2-day format with presentations + ignites + open spaces. ~300-500 attendees (typical DevOpsDays)
- **Key differentiator:** Half the day is Open Spaces (unconference), half is curated talks. Strong local Austin community focus.

## What They're Looking For

### 2026 Theme: "Value All The Way Down"

> How do we ensure that every process, every tool, and every team interaction is focused on delivering value to the customer?

**Specifically interested in:**

- Platform engineering and its role in value delivery
- SRE transitions: moving from reactive to proactive
- AI's impact on DevOps workflows and value delivery
- CI/CD pipeline evolution
- Impact of security and auditing processes on delivery
- How to foster collaboration to deliver value

### Talk Formats

- **Ignite talks:** 5 minutes, 20 auto-advancing slides (75% acceptance rate — easiest to get accepted)
- **Presentations:** 20 minutes
- **Workshops:** 90 minutes, ~35 attendees

### Submission Guidelines

- **Max 2 entries per person**
- No vendor/sales talks
- Prefer **local Austin speakers** (at least half the program)
- Prefer **new/unheard voices** — people who haven't presented extensively
- Prefer **original talks** not given elsewhere
- **Use the Notes section liberally** — tell them why you, why this talk, relevant experience
- Real-world experience stories preferred: "We had problem X; we did Y; here's what happened"

**Platform engineering was a dominant theme:**

- 3+ talks directly about platform engineering (Platform PM, Sociotechnical Platform Engineering, FaaS Platform)

**AI was well-represented but with DevOps angles:**

- AI incident resolution, local LLMs, AI networking challenges, AI in DevOps history
- Not pure AI coding talks — all had an operations/infrastructure angle

**Security was a recurring thread:**

- Vulnerability management, container security, JWTs, AWS security, OSS licensing
- Multiple open spaces on security topics

**Culture and human topics were valued:**

- Both keynotes were culture-focused (learning culture, egoless engineering)
- Your talk on team communication was accepted!
- Career transitions, personal growth, product thinking

**Real-world stories dominated:**

- Most talks followed "We had this problem → We did this → Here's what happened" format
- Datadog ARM migration, platform engineering transitions, infrastructure evolution stories

## Submission Recommendations

### 🏆 STRONG RECOMMENDATIONS

#### 1. How to Measure Developer Productivity

**Format:** Presentation (20 min)  
**Why it fits PERFECTLY:**

- Directly aligns with 2026 theme "Value All The Way Down" — measuring productivity = measuring value delivery
- 2025 had multiple talks about process improvement and team effectiveness
- DevOps audience cares deeply about metrics (DORA metrics, cycle time, lead time)
- Not a pure coding talk — it's about process and culture, which DevOpsDays values
- You can frame it as: "How do we know we're delivering value? By measuring what matters."

**Notes section tip:** Reference the 2026 theme directly. Frame productivity measurement as essential to knowing if you're delivering value. Mention speaking at DevOpsDays Austin 2025.

---

#### 2. The Copilot Gap: What You're Missing With AI Coding Assistants

**Format:** Presentation (20 min)  
**Why it fits:**

- 2026 theme calls out "AI's impact on DevOps workflows and value delivery"
- 2025 had 3+ AI-related sessions — clear appetite
- The "gap" angle aligns with value delivery — are AI tools actually delivering value, or are teams leaving value on the table?
- DevOps angle: how AI coding tools affect CI/CD, code quality, team workflows

**Positioning tip:** Frame this through the DevOps/value lens, not just developer productivity. Example: "Is AI actually accelerating your delivery pipeline, or just your typing speed?"
