## Topics They're Looking For

TTF is a Quality Assurance and Testing conference accepting proposals on topics related to **Product Quality — software, hardware, or beyond**. Listed themes include but are not limited to:

1. **AI in Test** — Using AI/ML to improve testing
2. **Testing AI** — How to test AI/ML systems
3. **Test Automation** — Frameworks, strategies, tools
4. **Mobile Testing** — Mobile-specific QA
5. **Tools** — Testing tools and ecosystems
6. **CI/CD** — Continuous integration/delivery and testing
7. **Metrics & Monitoring** — Quality metrics, observability
8. **Teamwork, Social, Personal Growth** — Soft skills, team dynamics, career growth
9. **Non-functional Testing** — Performance, security, accessibility, etc.
