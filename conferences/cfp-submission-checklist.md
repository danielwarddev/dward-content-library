## Submission Strategy Notes

### Talk Prioritization

- **Most Versatile:** "From Autocomplete to Autopilot: Mastering GitHub Copilot" (fits all AI conferences)
- **Second Most Versatile:** "GitHub Copilot: Mind The Gap" (fits 5/6 conferences)
- **AI-Specific:** "AI + .NET: Microsoft Agent Framework" (fits all AI conferences)
- **Testing-Focused:** "TestContainers" or "xUnit Expanded" (best for Scenic City Summit)

### Tips for Success

- **AI Coding Summit:** Focus on one core message, 5-min video intro, virtual presentation
- **Scenic City Summit:** Broader tech audience, regional appeal, in-person presentation
- **TransformConf:** First-year conference, AI/JetBrains focus, international audience
- **Xtreme Conferences:** Include video, intermediate/advanced level, can submit to multiple
