## ⚠️ ORGANIZER TIPS (From ai-coding-summit-tips.md) - APPLY TO THIS CONFERENCE

### Content Preparation

-   **Focus on ONE Core Message** - Convey one main idea/takeaway to avoid overwhelming
-   **Tell a Story** - Weave narrative throughout; using humor helps
-   **Use Analogies** - Simplify complex concepts
-   **Support with Data** - But don't overload slides with numbers; use visuals

### Talk Structure

-   **Introduction:** Start with a hook, brief intro, outline
-   **Body:** Clear sections with examples, case studies, or demos
-   **Conclusion:** Summarize key points, end with strong message, offer actionable takeaways

### Slide Design

-   **Keep It Simple** - Minimal text, one idea per slide, bullet points sparingly
-   **Visuals Are Key** - Use diagrams, charts, images, infographics
-   **Readable Text** - Large enough for smaller screens (important for code examples!)
-   **Minimal Animation** - Transitions can get lost in streaming

### Virtual-Specific Tips (CRITICAL - This is an online conference!)

-   **Stable Internet Connection**
-   **Good Lighting** - Position light source in front of you
-   **Good microphone/headset**
-   **Declutter background** or use virtual background
-   **Mute notifications, close windows**
-   **Be more animated** than in-person due to virtual format
-   **Have backup plan** for technical issues

### Speaking & Delivery

-   **Practice multiple times**
-   **Clear and concise language** - Avoid jargon
-   **Don't rush** - Moderate pace with pauses for emphasis
-   **Modulate your voice** - Vary tone, pitch, volume
-   **Be your natural self**
