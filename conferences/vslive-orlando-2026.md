## Conference Profile

- **Running for 30+ years** — one of the longest-running Microsoft-focused developer conferences
- **Part of Live! 360 Tech Con** — 6 co-located conferences at one event for one price
- **Vibe:** Enterprise-focused training conference with a heavy Microsoft ecosystem bent. Deep hands-on learning, not a "cool kids" community conf. Expect enterprise developers sent by their companies (ticket prices are $1,925-$3,225).
- **Tagline:** "Code in Focus"
- **Audience:** Full-stack engineers, cloud-native architects, enterprise developers on the Microsoft stack
- Premium resort venue at Universal Orlando — family-friendly "tech-cation" positioning
