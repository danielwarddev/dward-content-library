## Conference Vibe & Size

- **Nonprofit, community-run** event — "led by developers for developers"
- **Regional focus**: Northeast US, within ~2h drive of NYC, Philadelphia, northern NJ, and within reach of Boston and DC
- Deliberately **inclusive and welcoming** — "TechBash is for everyone"
- Affordable compared to national conferences
- Venue is a resort with 220,000 sq ft indoor waterpark (largest in Northeast US) — very family-friendly
- Hotel rooms at $169/night (discounted), includes waterpark access for up to 4 people
- Casual dress code
- Meals included: hot breakfast, full lunch daily, afternoon snacks, light appetizers at evening events
- Social events: Welcome Reception, Game Night
- 5 breakout rooms, ~70 total sessions across 3 conference days
- Speaker sponsors in 2025 included: Redgate, Uno Platform, Geneca
