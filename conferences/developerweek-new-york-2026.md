## Session Categories (8 Summits)

DeveloperWeek NY 2026 features **eight distinct conferences organized as Summits**, all part of a combined CFP:

### 1. AI DevSummit

Applying AI and machine learning across products and organizations, from LLM integration and intelligent workflows to model training, optimization, and deployment. Delivers the latest insights about the biggest digital innovation since the public launch of the internet — learn how to put AI to work in your own products and across your organization, whether integrating LLM chatbots into development/support flows, creating custom AI-driven experiences for customers, or digging deep into model training and performance optimization.

### 2. API & Microservices Summit

Designing, building, and operating modern APIs and distributed services across REST, GraphQL, gRPC, Protobuf, MCP, and emerging protocols at the intersection of AI, Cloud, and Data.

### 3. Cloud-Native Summit

Building and deploying cloud-native applications using containers, Kubernetes, virtualization, and cross-platform infrastructure to run software anywhere at scale. No matter your skill level, find experts and peers who share knowledge and experiences to shortcut your educational journey.

### 4. Dev Innovation Summit

Exploring emerging technologies, paradigm shifts, and forward-looking ideas that shape the future of software, separating real innovation from short-term hype. A showcase where thought leaders share visions of what's next and technical practitioners demo the future.

### 5. DevExec Summit

Leadership strategies for technical organizations, including hiring and retaining engineering talent, managing teams and products, and aligning technology with business outcomes. Covers technical skills and business leadership for CTOs, engineering managers, and other digital leaders.

### 6. DevOps Summit

Operating reliable, scalable systems through CI/CD, platform engineering, infrastructure as code, SRE practices, and automated delivery pipelines. Whether full Platform Engineering paradigm with internal developer portals and automated CI/CD, or deploying custom stacks on esoteric hardware.

### 7. DevSecurity Summit

Protecting applications, data, and infrastructure through secure development practices, vulnerability management, and modern security tooling across the software lifecycle. Covers vulnerability discovery, communicating fixes, and managing all aspects of data and infrastructure security.

### 8. Product Summit

Building successful digital products through strong product management, customer insight, roadmap execution, and close collaboration between product and engineering teams. Best practices from PMs who understand customers and work directly with engineering to deliver measurable results.
