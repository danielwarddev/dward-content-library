# NDC Oslo 2026

## Conference Details

-   **Event:** NDC Oslo 2026
-   **Dates:** September 14-18, 2026 (5 days: workshops + conference)
-   **Location:** Oslo Spektrum, Oslo, Norway
-   **Website:** https://ndcoslo.com
-   **CFP Link:** https://sessionize.com/ndc-oslo-2026
-   **CFP Deadline:** April 19, 2026

## Speaker Benefits

-   ✅ Travel expenses covered (non-local speakers)
-   ✅ Accommodation covered (non-local speakers)
-   ✅ 3-day conference pass
-   ⚠️ No travel/accommodation for lightning talks, co-speakers, or local speakers

## Conference Profile

-   **Focus:** The flagship NDC event — broad software development topics with strong .NET heritage
-   **Size/vibe:** Large (thousands of attendees), premier European developer conference
-   **Prestige:** One of the most respected developer conferences in Europe

## Talk Formats

-   **Regular talks:** 60 minutes
-   **Lightning talks:** 10 minutes
-   **Workshops:** 60-120 minutes (1 or 2 talk slots)
-   **Pre-Conference Workshops:** 2 days

## What They're Looking For

> "If you have something to say, then speak up!"

**Submission guidance:**

-   Limit submissions to 3-4 talks (your best ones, not your entire portfolio)
-   Include links to videos, slides, or blog posts if you have speaking experience

### 💡 SUBMISSION STRATEGY

NDC Oslo is competitive — it's the flagship event. For your 3-4 submissions, I'd recommend:

1. **The Copilot Gap** — Perfect fit for "Coding with AI / Vibe coding"
2. **No More SQLite / TestContainers** — Strong testing content with demo
3. **Getting Started With AI / Agent Framework** — Hits both .NET and the hot AI/Agents topic
4. **One wildcard:** Either Stryker (testing depth) or Measure Developer Productivity (appeals to different track)

### 🎯 CONSIDER: Workshop Submission

Given NDC Oslo's workshop format (60-120 min or 2-day pre-conference), you could potentially expand one of your talks into a workshop:

-   **TestContainers Workshop** — Hands-on integration testing with containers
-   **AI for .NET Devs Workshop** — Deeper dive into building AI-powered apps

Workshops have less competition than regular talks and provide more value to attendees.
