## Pro Tips

### Packaging for Ground Advantage (2-5 days in transit)

Since Ground takes longer, packaging matters more:

1. **Cards:** Top loaders → team bags → bubble wrap → rigid mailer inside box
2. **ETBs/Booster Boxes:** Original packaging → bubble wrap → snug box fit
3. **Booster Packs:** Hard case/acrylic → bubble wrap → padded envelope in box
4. **Fill ALL empty space** with packing paper or air pillows

### Save Money

1. **Buy supplies in bulk:** Boxes, bubble mailers, tape from ULINE or Amazon
2. **Use USPS.com:** ~5-10% cheaper than Post Office counter rates
3. **Schedule free pickups:** USPS will pick up from your home
4. **Combine shipments:** Same buyer = one box when possible

### Reduce Risk

1. **Photograph everything** before shipping
2. **Video yourself packing** high-value items
3. **Keep all receipts** for insurance claims
4. **Require signature** for anything over $300
