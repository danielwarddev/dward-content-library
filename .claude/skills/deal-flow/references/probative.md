# 1. The Probative Conversation

**Job:** be seen as an expert worth talking to, *before* any opportunity exists.

**Not** a sales conversation. Nothing is being sold, qualified, or priced. The only goal is that the
right people come to see you as the person who understands their problem.

## What It Looks Like

Demonstrating expertise in public, repeatedly, without asking for anything:

- Conference talks and user group sessions
- Blog posts and documentation sites
- Podcasts and guest appearances
- Free tools
- Genuinely useful answers in community spaces

## Why This Seller Is Unusually Strong Here

Most independents spend years trying to get their first Probative conversations. This one already has:

| Asset | Probative value |
| ----- | --------------- |
| Microsoft MVP | Third-party validated expertise, renewed annually |
| ~25 conference talks | Hundreds of people have watched an hour of demonstrated skill |
| Monthly .NET user group | Recurring, local, high-trust |
| daninacan.com | Dated, public body of work |
| testingwithdot.net | (Once live) a whole site as proof |

**This conversation is not the bottleneck. Do not add more of it as a way to avoid Conversation 2.**

## The Only Thing Missing: The Handoff

A Probative conversation is wasted if it doesn't create a path to a Qualifying conversation.

Current leak: talks end, people clap, everyone leaves. No capture, no CTA, no follow-up.

**Fixes, in order of leverage:**

1. **A closing slide with one link** (danielward.dev). Not "thanks!" — one line naming who the workshop
   is for and where to find it.
2. **Individual follow-ups within a week.** ~8 messages after each user group. This is the actual
   transition to Conversation 2.
3. **Email capture** so the Probative relationship persists beyond one room.
4. **A recorded talk clip on the landing page** — a Probative conversation that runs while asleep.

## Coaching Notes

- **The temptation to guard against:** doing more Probative work because it's comfortable and
  legible-feeling. Writing another post is easier than asking a named person a direct question.
  More Probative output does not fix a Qualifying deficit.
- **Selling from the stage doesn't work and isn't needed.** Especially at Microsoft-sponsored events.
  The talk earns the right to a follow-up message; that message does the selling.
- **Probative material should show judgment, not just syntax.** Opinionated content ("which of these
  four assertion libraries, and why") demonstrates expertise. Reference material demonstrates literacy.

## Exit Criteria

Move to Qualifying when someone signals a problem — asks a question about their own situation,
describes a struggle, or replies to a referral ask. That signal is an invitation. Take it the same day.
