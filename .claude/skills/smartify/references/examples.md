# Examples

## Quick Before/After

**Bad goal:** I want to get healthy.

> **SMART goal:** I want to be able to do two consecutive pull-ups by the end of the year by practicing at the gym three times per week.

---

**Bad goal:** I want to improve my performance.

> **SMART goal:** Improve PowerPoint skills by taking online classes so it only requires 25% of work time (down from 40%) within six months.

---

**Bad goal:** I want to learn a foreign language.

> **SMART goal:** In two months, I will be able to conduct a 15-minute basic business introduction in Spanish by completing an online course and practicing for 20 minutes daily, which aligns with our department's initiative to better support our international client base.

## In-Depth Example (30-Minute Rule)

**Initial goal:** I want to complete a project.

**30-minute check:** This goal is far too large for 30 minutes. Extracted sub-task: *Draft the mobile app requirements doc.*

**SMART goal:** Draft the mobile app requirements document by end of day today, covering the three core user flows identified in this week's customer feedback, so the dev team has a clear scope to review in tomorrow's kickoff meeting.

- **Deadline:** End of day today.
