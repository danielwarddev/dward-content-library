# Phase 2: Idea Validation (CRITICAL)

**After generating each idea, run it through validation checks. If an idea fails validation, abandon it and generate a replacement.**

## Validation Signals to Hunt For

For each idea, actively search for evidence that people would pay:

### 1. Existing Spend Signals

- Are people already paying for inferior solutions?
- Are there Upwork/Fiverr gigs solving this manually?
- Are companies hiring roles to handle this problem?

### 2. Urgency Signals (The "Well" Test)

- Can you find 5-10 specific people who would use a crappy MVP today?
- Are there "shut up and take my money" comments anywhere?
- Is anyone asking "when will X be ready" or "does X exist yet"?

### 3. Frequency of Problem

- Is this a daily/weekly pain or a once-a-year annoyance?
- Would solving this become embedded in their workflow?

### 4. User Conversation Evidence

- Find and document real user conversations about this problem
- Quote actual users describing their frustration
- Note emotional intensity (mild annoyance vs. genuine anger)

---

## Kill Criteria (Abandon If...)

🛑 **Immediately abandon an idea if:**

- **No existing spend:** Nobody is currently paying to solve this (even badly)
- **Crater pattern:** Lots of "that's cool" but no urgent need
- **Can't find 5 real users:** Unable to identify 5-10 specific people who'd use MVP
- **Problem is too rare:** Happens once a year, not worth automating
- **Competition is too strong:** Dominated by well-funded players with no clear angle
- **No access to customers:** Can't figure out how to reach and talk to them
- **Schlep without reward:** It's hard to build AND the market is small

---

## Continue Signals (Proceed If...)

✅ **Strong proceed signals:**

- Unprompted "I would pay for this" comments found online
- People actively asking if solutions exist
- High-upvoted complaint threads with intense frustration
- Companies hiring people to solve this manually
- Existing spend on freelancers, consultants, or bad software
- Clear path to reach first 25 customers

---

## Validation Output Per Idea

For each idea that passes initial scoring, document:

1. **User Voices:** 3-5 real quotes from people experiencing this problem
2. **Spend Evidence:** How are people currently paying to solve this?
3. **First 10 Customers:** Specific communities, companies, or personas to target
4. **PMF Stage Entry:** What stage would you enter, and is that viable?
5. **Well or Crater:** Is this narrow+deep or broad+shallow appeal?
6. **Validation Verdict:** ✅ Validated / ⚠️ Weak signals / 🛑 Failed

**If an idea receives 🛑 Failed, do not include it in final output. Generate a replacement idea instead.**
