# Social Post Style Guide

Reference existing posts in `speaking-content/user-group/` for examples of phrasing, structure, and marketing style.

## Writing Voice

The hook line should sound like a real person wrote it. Study previous posts in `speaking-content/user-group/` to match the tone. Common patterns:

- A simple question: "Are you using health checks in .NET?"
- A direct statement about the event: "First .NET@Noon of the year is next week!"
- A plain description of what attendees will learn: "Learn how to practice reactive UI programming in .NET using ReactiveUI"

Keep the hook line to one or two sentences. Do not oversell.

## Avoid Typical AI-Generated Language

Including but not limited to:

- Em dashes; use commas, periods, or "and" instead
- "Dive into" or "deep dive"
- "Not just X, it's Y" or "It's not about X, it's about Y"
- "Unlock", "unleash", "supercharge", "revolutionize", or "game-changer"
- "Elevate your", "level up your", or "take your X to the next level"
- "In the world of" or "in today's landscape"
- Overly punchy or marketing-heavy sentence fragments

Keep it conversational, straightforward, and human.

## General Style

- Start with a catchy hook or question related to the topic.
- Use relevant hashtags: always `#dotnet` plus one or two researched tags.
- Use emojis for visual structure:
  - 🎤 or 🎙 for the talk title
  - 👨‍🏫 or 👨‍💼 for the speaker
  - ⌚ or 📆 for the date and time
  - 🌐 for the location
- End with "Signup:" or "Sign up here:" followed by the Meetup link.
- For in-person events, add 🗣️ at the start and mention food or drinks when applicable.
