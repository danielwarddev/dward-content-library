### Offerings

#### 🔧 GitHub Copilot Workshops
Help your team get productive with GitHub Copilot fast. Covers setup, best practices, agent mode, MCP, and custom instructions. Available in-person or remote, half-day or full-day format.

#### 🏫 Custom Team Training
Tailored training sessions on .NET development, testing strategies, mocking, developer tooling, or AI-assisted development. Built around your team's actual codebase and pain points.

#### 🎤 Speaking Engagements
Available for conferences, meetups, and company events. See my [Speaking page](https://daninacan.com/speaking/) for current topics and past talks.

#### 🤝 Consulting & Advisory
Short-term advisory for teams adopting AI tooling or leveling up their development practices. Architecture reviews, dev process improvement, toolchain recommendations.

#### 🧑‍💻 1:1 Coaching & Mentoring
For individual developers who want to level up — whether that's testing skills, .NET tooling, career trajectory, or getting started with AI-assisted development.

#### 🔍 Code Reviews & Audits
A thorough review of your codebase, focused on testing coverage, code quality, architecture, and tooling. Includes a written summary of findings and recommendations.

## Additional Notes

- **Pricing:** Intentionally left off here — "contact to discuss" is standard for custom services like these; listing prices often anchors low or scares off good leads.
- **Social proof:** Consider adding 1–2 short testimonial quotes once you have them. Even just a company name/logo strip helps.
- **Keep it scannable:** Don't write paragraphs for each offering. Short bullets or one-liner descriptions work best — visitors are evaluating fit quickly.
