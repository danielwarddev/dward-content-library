## Known Online Resources

Unfortunately, there is **no single definitive, actively maintained list** of failed tech CEO predictions online. However, several articles and sites cover this topic in pieces:

### Articles & Listicles

1. **"The Worst Tech Predictions of All Time"** — Various outlets (Digital Trends, Network World, etc.) have published versions of this listicle over the years, but they tend to focus on *historical* bad predictions (e.g., "640K ought to be enough for anybody" — Bill Gates, though this quote is likely apocryphal). They don't typically track *modern* CEO promises with timelines.

2. **Elon Musk Self-Driving Timeline Trackers** — Several outlets have tracked Musk's repeated self-driving promises specifically:
   - The Washington Post, Ars Technica, and CNBC have all published "timeline of Elon Musk's self-driving promises" articles
   - Search for: *"elon musk self-driving timeline promises"* for current versions

3. **AI Hype Prediction Trackers** — With the AI boom, several people have started tracking failed AI predictions:
   - **Gary Marcus** (NYU professor, AI skeptic) regularly catalogs failed AI predictions on his Substack: https://garymarcus.substack.com/
   - **Emily Bender** (University of Washington) tracks overpromising in AI
   - **AI Snake Oil** (Arvind Narayanan & Sayash Kapoor, Princeton) — a Substack & book specifically about AI hype: https://www.aisnakeoil.com/

4. **"The AI Index Report"** by Stanford HAI — Annual report that includes actual benchmarks vs. hype claims: https://aiindex.stanford.edu/

5. **Metaculus & Prediction Markets** — Sites like Metaculus (https://www.metaculus.com/) track tech predictions with probability estimates. You can look back at resolved predictions to find which ones failed.

6. **"Big Technology" Newsletter** by Alex Kantrowitz** — Occasionally covers broken tech promises: https://www.bigtechnology.com/

## Broader Historical Predictions (Classic List)

These are the commonly cited "worst tech predictions ever" (often by executives, not necessarily CEOs):

- **Ken Olsen, DEC founder (1977):** "There is no reason anyone would want a computer in their home."
- **Steve Ballmer, Microsoft CEO (2007):** "There's no chance that the iPhone is going to get any significant market share."
- **Darryl Zanuck, 20th Century Fox (1946):** "Television won't be able to hold onto any market it captures after the first six months."
- **Thomas Watson, IBM President (1943):** "I think there is a world market for maybe five computers."
- **Robert Metcalfe (1995):** Predicted the internet would "catastrophically collapse" in 1996.
- **Paul Krugman (1998):** "By 2005 or so, it will become clear that the Internet's impact on the economy has been no greater than the fax machine's."

## Suggestion: A Dedicated Tracker Would Be Valuable

There doesn't appear to be anyone maintaining a **living, updated database** of tech CEO predictions mapped to outcomes. This could be a great blog post or even a small web project:

- Track prediction, who made it, date, claimed timeline, source link, and actual outcome
- Focus specifically on *modern* tech CEO promises (post-2010) with specific timelines
- Could be a GitHub repo, a simple website, or a recurring blog post series

This would fill a real gap — the Gary Marcus Substack and AI Snake Oil come closest for AI-specific claims, but nobody seems to be doing it across all of tech.
