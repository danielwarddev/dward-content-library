# TransformConf 2026

## Conference Details

-   **Name:** TransformConf 2026
-   **Dates:** September 15-16, 2026
-   **Location:** Tobacco Dock, London, United Kingdom
-   **Organizer:** JetBrains
-   **CFP Deadline:** June 12, 2026 (11:59 PM GMT+1)
-   **CFP Link:** https://sessionize.com/transformconf/?e=508868
-   **Website:** https://transformconf.ai
-   **Session Formats:** 45-minute talks (full sessions) or 15-minute lightning talks
-   **Travel/Accommodation:** Covered for full sessions only (one speaker per talk)

## Conference Focus

TransformConf is a **brand new** developer-first conference hosted by JetBrains, focused on the **practical use of AI in software development**. This is its first edition ever - no past programs to reference.

The conference aims to encourage "open, technical, and honest discussion among practitioners who are shaping what it means to build software with AI."

## Target Audience

-   Engineers and developers
-   ML practitioners and researchers
-   DevOps specialists
-   Technical leaders and architects
-   Developer experience/productivity engineers
-   Product-minded developers working with AI in production

## Session Categories/Topics

1. **AI application development and engineering** - Building, deploying, and maintaining AI systems with focus on end-to-end AI application designs
2. **AI product development** - Collaboration, product thinking, and aligning AI development across disciplines
3. **Advanced modeling and training techniques** - Technical underpinnings of AI systems, modeling techniques, and performance
4. **AI agents and developer experience** - Rethinking productivity, roles, and human-AI collaboration in software development
5. **Developer education and skills** - Helping professionals adapt and develop skills in the "AI era"
6. **Future of software and work** - Long-term changes to programming, teams, and the software industry
7. **Moving beyond the hype** - Separating myths from reality, challenging AGI narratives, regulations/compliance, practical approaches to AI systems

## CFP Tips from Organizers

-   **Title:** Make it catchy but accurate and consistent with abstract
-   **Abstract:** Keep concise (200-400 words), clearly describe content and key takeaways
-   **Differentiation:** For popular topics (especially AI agents), clearly explain what sets your talk apart
-   **Case studies:** Include specific numbers and metrics if presenting a case study
-   **Level:** Accurately define the level of your talk
-   **Category:** Choose the first relevant category in the dropdown
