## Conference Size & Vibe

- **Size:** "Hundreds of global expected attendees" per the CFP — this is a **focused, vendor-hosted user conference**, not a massive community conference. Think 500–1,000 range. swampUP 2025 was held in Napa Valley; 2026 moves to NYC for likely larger attendance.
- **Audience:** "Top-flight roles and positions" — developers, architects, security professionals, DevOps engineers, and leadership who use or evaluate JFrog products
- **Vibe:** Premium vendor conference. High production value. Mix of JFrog product keynotes and community/customer breakout sessions. Heavy on supply chain security, artifact management, and AI/MLOps. The 2025 event featured keynotes from JFrog leadership plus partners (NVIDIA, Sonar, ServiceNow).
- **11th year** of the conference (10th annual was in 2025 per Forrester)
- **Sponsor/Partner presence:** Significant — the 2025 event had multiple sponsors and partner integrations showcased

### 2025 Session Examples (for tone/style reference)

## Key Observations

1. **Strong JFrog ecosystem focus** — Many 2025 talks integrated with or demonstrated JFrog products. Talks don't need to BE about JFrog, but showing how your topic connects to the supply chain/artifact management world will help.
2. **AI is a major theme** — AI/MLOps, agentic DevOps, and AI-driven workflows were prominent in 2025 and are explicitly called out for 2026.
3. **Practitioner-first** — The LinkedIn post is crystal clear: they want in-production war stories, not theory. Real examples, real lessons.
4. **Security-heavy** — Supply chain security, DevSecOps, SBOM, compliance are core themes throughout.
5. **Relatively small and intimate** — This isn't KubeCon (10K+). Expect quality networking in a focused group.
