# Louisville .NET Meetup (LouDotNet)

## Event Details

-   **Event:** Louisville .NET Meetup
-   **Type:** Monthly meetup (3rd Thursday of each month)
-   **Time:** 6:30 PM EST/EDT (doors at 6:00)
-   **Location:** Louisville, Kentucky (in-person + online via Twitch/YouTube)
-   **Website:** https://www.meetup.com/louisville-dotnet/
-   **CFP Link:** https://sessionize.com/loudotnet
-   **CFP Status:** Always open (rolling submissions through January 2027)

## Speaker Benefits

-   ✅ Free to attend
-   ✅ Food provided by sponsors
-   ✅ Post-meetup social at nearby restaurant
-   ✅ Virtual presentations accepted (streamed on Twitch/YouTube)
-   ✅ First-time speaker coaching available

## Meetup Profile

-   **Focus:** .NET technology stack
-   **Size/vibe:** Local community meetup, welcoming atmosphere
-   **Format:** In-person + livestreamed online
-   **Audience:** .NET developers in the Louisville area (and online viewers)

## What They're Looking For

> "We are looking for speakers eager to share their knowledge about topics of interest to .NET developers. We are looking for both experienced speakers but also first-time speakers."

**Key notes:**

-   Virtual presentations welcome for those who can't travel
-   They help first-time speakers with presentation details and coaching
-   Social atmosphere — dinner after the meetup
