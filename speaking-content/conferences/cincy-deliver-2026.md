## Conference Profile

- **Focus:** Delivering value through quality software — agile process, technical content, and software delivery best practices
- **Size:** 450+ attendees, 6 tracks (up to 8 rooms including sponsor rooms)
- **Vibe:** Community-run, non-profit, Midwest regional conference. Cincinnati's largest and longest-running non-profit conference for software development teams. Formerly "Cincinnati Day of Agile" (rebranded 2021). Elegant venue at The Manor House.
- **Mission:** "Help teams deliver real value by building and maintaining high-quality software"
- **Tagline:** "Delivering Value Through Quality Software"
- **History:** Started in 2010 as a single-track agile event; grown into a six-track conference blending agile, technical, and delivery content

## Target Audience

AI practitioners, agilists, Scrum Masters, Product Owners, Program Managers, Business Analysts, Developers, Engineers, QA & Testing Professionals, DevOps Engineers, and Cloud Engineers.

## What They're Looking For

> "Talks covering the full spectrum of delivering quality software"

The conference spans both **process/agile** and **technical** content. Based on past sessions, they accept a broad mix:

- Agile transformation stories and experiences
- Technical deep-dives (Azure, cloud, architecture, DevOps)
- AI/ML practical applications
- Soft skills and leadership (psychological safety, emotional intelligence)
- Software quality and testing
- Mobile and web development practices
- Security practices

The audience level skews **Introduction to Intermediate** — this is a community conference, not a deep expert conference.
