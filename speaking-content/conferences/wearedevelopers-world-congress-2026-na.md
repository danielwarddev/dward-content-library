# WeAreDevelopers World Congress 2026 - North America

## Conference Details

-   **Event:** WeAreDevelopers World Congress 2026 - North America
-   **Dates:** September 23-25, 2026
-   **Location:** San Jose Convention Center, San Jose, California
-   **Website:** https://wearedevelopers.us
-   **CFP Link:** https://sessionize.com/wearedevelopers-world-congress-2026-us
-   **CFP Deadline:** March 31, 2026

## Conference Profile

-   **Focus:** Enterprise-scale software development, AI innovation, and tech leadership
-   **Size/vibe:** Massive (10,000+ developers), flagship global event with big-name speakers (Steve Wozniak, Tim Berners-Lee, Scott Hanselman, etc.)
-   **Talk formats:** 20+ stages, tech talks, live coding, workshops
-   **Speaker perks:** Free full-access pass, VIP lounge, speakers reception, professional recording

## What They're Looking For

From the CFP page:

> ✅ What Makes a Standout Session?
>
> -   🔝 **Real-World Value:** Talks that solve real problems with deep technical insights, clever patterns, or battle-tested use cases.
> -   🔝 **Fresh Ideas:** Sessions that introduce a novel technology, a fresh perspective on a common problem, or a look into the future of tech.
> -   🔝 **Engaging Delivery:** We love dynamic speakers! Live coding is always a huge plus, as are talks that entertain and inspire.
> -   🔝 **Language:** All sessions must be presented in English.

## Session Categories

### AI Categories (High Priority)

-   **AI - Applied AI & Use Cases** — Practical applications of AI: real-world integrations, product features, and industry case studies.
-   **AI - Engineering & Platforms** — Building and operating AI systems: pipelines, MLOps, LLMOps, RAG, agent frameworks, and infrastructure.
-   **AI - Models** — Core algorithms and models: machine learning, deep learning, LLMs, training, fine-tuning, and evaluation.
-   **AI - Responsible & Safe AI** — Making AI trustworthy: ethics, bias, safety, governance, regulation, and compliance.

### Development Categories

-   **Backend & APIs** — Server-side development: frameworks, API design, microservices, scalability, and performance.
-   **Frontend & Web** — Everything in the browser: frameworks, JavaScript/TypeScript, performance, accessibility, and UX.
-   **.NET Ecosystem** — Everything in the .NET world: C#, ASP.NET, runtime, frameworks, performance, and tooling.
-   **Java & JVM** — The JVM ecosystem: Java, Kotlin, Scala, frameworks (Spring, Quarkus, Micronaut), performance, and tooling.
-   **Programming Languages & Paradigms** — Language design and paradigms: compilers, runtimes, type systems, new languages, and language evolution.
-   **Systems Programming** — Low-level development: C/C++/Rust/Go, OS internals, performance, and concurrency.
-   **Mobile & Cross-Platform** — Building for mobile: Android, iOS, Flutter, React Native, multiplatform development, and tooling.

### Infrastructure & Operations

-   **Cloud & Infrastructure** — Working with the cloud: cloud strategies, multi-cloud, serverless, infra patterns, IaC, networking, and scaling.
-   **DevOps & Platform Engineering** — Running and automating systems: CI/CD, GitOps, observability, SRE, platform engineering, and automation.
-   **Data Engineering & Databases** — Managing and processing data: pipelines, streaming, analytics, SQL/NoSQL, lakehouse, and performance.

### Quality & Architecture

-   **Testing, QA & Reliability** — Ensuring quality: testing strategies, automation, CI/CD quality gates, chaos, and reliability engineering.
-   **Software Architecture & System Design** — Designing software at scale: distributed systems, architecture trade-offs, EDA, patterns, and integration.
-   **Security, Privacy & Compliance** — Protecting systems and data: secure coding, DevSecOps, threat modeling, privacy, and compliance.

### Leadership & Culture

-   **DevEx & Productivity** — Improving how developers work: workflows, tooling, docs, internal platforms, and productivity practices.
-   **Engineering Leadership & Culture** — Leading engineering teams: agile practices, engineering culture, scaling teams, collaboration, and motivation.
-   **Leadership, Strategy & Industry Trends** — Executive-level insights: CxO perspectives, digital transformation, business strategy, and market outlook.
-   **People & Culture** — Talent and recruiting, organizational culture, diversity & inclusion, well-being, and workplace transformation.

### Other Categories

-   **DevRel & Advocacy** — Building and supporting communities: DevRel, evangelism, advocacy, education, and engagement.
-   **Product Management & Innovation** — Building great products: discovery, delivery, product strategy, AI-native products, and innovation practices.
-   **Product Growth & Marketing** — Growing products and markets: go-to-market strategies, user acquisition, retention, monetization, and growth tactics.
-   **Startups & Entrepreneurship** — Founding and scaling companies: startup journeys, fundraising, growth challenges, and lessons from entrepreneurs.
-   **Game Development & Technology** — Game tech: engines, graphics, VR/AR/XR, real-time rendering, tools, and workflows.
-   **IoT, Edge & Robotics** — Connected devices and autonomy: IoT, embedded systems, edge runtimes, robotics, and autonomous systems.
-   **Blockchain & Web3** — Decentralized technologies: protocols, smart contracts, dApps, infrastructure, and adoption stories.
-   **Quantum Computing** — Computing beyond classical: quantum algorithms, languages, frameworks, simulators, and hybrid workflows.

## Submission Recommendations

### 🏆 STRONG RECOMMENDATIONS (Existing Talks)

#### 1. The Copilot Gap: What You're Missing With AI Coding Assistants

**Best fit category:** AI - Applied AI & Use Cases  
**Why it fits well:**

-   AI is clearly their #1 topic priority (4 AI-specific categories!)
-   Practical, real-world focus on AI tools aligns with "Real-World Value" preference
-   Not another "intro to AI" talk — offers a unique angle on effective AI usage
-   Addresses the "responsible" side without being preachy

**💡 Suggestion:** Consider strengthening the abstract with a more provocative "startling sentence" — perhaps a stat like "Developers using Copilot incorrectly spend 20% _more_ time on tasks than those without it."

---

#### 2. Getting Started With AI For .NET Devs With Agent Framework

**Best fit category:** AI - Engineering & Platforms (covers "agent frameworks") or .NET Ecosystem  
**Why it fits well:**

-   Agent frameworks are explicitly called out in their categories
-   .NET has its own dedicated track
-   Live coding demo aligns with their preferences
-   Timely topic given the surge in AI agent development

---

#### 3. No More SQLite - How to Write Tests With EF Core Using TestContainers

**Best fit category:** Testing, QA & Reliability  
**Why it fits well:**

-   Testing has a dedicated track (underrepresented at many conferences)
-   Practical, hands-on, solves a real pain point
-   Live coding demo is exactly what they want
-   Title is catchy with a clear hook

---

#### 4. How to Measure Developer Productivity

**Best fit category:** DevEx & Productivity OR Engineering Leadership & Culture  
**Why it fits well:**

-   Developer productivity is _the_ hot topic in engineering leadership
-   Appeals to both ICs and managers/leaders
-   Controversial enough to draw interest
-   No live coding needed — the topic carries itself
