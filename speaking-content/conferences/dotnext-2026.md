## Conference Profile

- **Focus:** .NET development — the largest and oldest .NET conference in Russia
- **Size:** ~800 participants, ~35 speakers, 30+ talks
- **Vibe:** Deep technical .NET content — strong emphasis on low-level internals and "how it works under the hood," plus practical everyday dev content
- **Format:** In-person (offline), 2-day conference
- **Language:** Primarily Russian (all 2025 talks were marked "In Russian")
- **Halls:** 4 concurrent tracks
- **History:** Running since 2014, 150+ conferences from the organizer

## ⚠️ Important Considerations

- **Language:** Content appears to be primarily delivered in Russian
- **Audience:** Primarily Russian-speaking .NET developers

## Selection Criteria

Proposals are evaluated on 6 dimensions:

1.  **Relevance** — Useful today and in the future, matches conference theme
2.  **Depth** — Reveals the subject deeply and comprehensively (no "Hello World" unless new/promising tech)
3.  **Originality** — Novel content or well-known topic presented in a different light
4.  **Expertise** — Speaker has hands-on experience and completed projects in the field
5.  **Practical applicability** — Covers existing problems/solutions AND shares personal experience
6.  **Speaking experience** — Prior conference/meetup experience is a "great advantage"; first-timers should expect rehearsals

## Assessment for Submission

2.  **Language:** The conference appears to be overwhelmingly Russian-language. All 2025 talks were marked "In Russian"

### Potential Fit (if language/travel were not barriers)

The technical content strongly aligns with .NET-focused talks. Topics like testing, AI for .NET, and performance would fit their categories well. However, the practical barriers around location and language make this conference **not recommended** for submission at this time.
