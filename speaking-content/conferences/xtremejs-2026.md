## Conference Description

XtremeJS is a global online conference for JavaScript enthusiasts, professionals, and researchers. It's built for developers who want impactful learning without the fluff, delivering 30-minute sessions packed with actionable knowledge.

The conference covers the full breadth of JavaScript:

-   Language advancements and runtime performance
-   Frameworks and tooling
-   Large-scale application architecture
-   Emerging trends like AI-powered development
-   Front-end, back-end, and full-stack development

## Organizer-Provided Tips (from xtreme-talk-tips)

### How to Get Accepted:

1. **Target intermediate/advanced level** - The committee prefers talks for experienced developers over beginner content
2. **Include a short video recording** - This helps the committee assess your presentation skills
3. **Compelling title** - Create intrigue and make attendees want to learn more
4. **Clear takeaways** - What will attendees walk away knowing or being able to do?
5. **Story-based abstract** - Frame your content as a journey or narrative
6. **Submit multiple proposals** - Increases chances of acceptance
7. **Consider co-presenting** - Can reduce pressure and bring diverse perspectives

### Common Mistakes to Avoid:

1. **Unclear abstract** - Be specific about what you'll cover
2. **Undefined audience** - Clearly state who the talk is for
3. **Too promotional** - Focus on teaching, not selling
4. **Missing key details** - Include technical depth and concrete examples
5. **Generic topics** - Stand out with unique angles or experiences

### Tips for Success (if accepted):

-   Engage with the virtual audience through Q&A
-   Have clear, readable slides for online viewing
-   Practice with virtual presentation tools
-   Keep energy high despite no in-person audience
