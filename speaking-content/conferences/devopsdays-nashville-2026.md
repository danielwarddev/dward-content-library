# DevOpsDays Nashville 2026

## Conference Details

-   **Event:** DevOpsDays Nashville 2026
-   **Dates:** May 14-15, 2026 (2 days)
-   **Location:** Middle Tennessee State University (MTSU), Murfreesboro, TN
-   **Website:** https://devopsdays.org/events/2026-nashville/welcome/
-   **CFP Link:** https://www.papercall.io/devopsdaysnashville2026
-   **CFP Deadline:** February 27, 2026
-   **Expected attendance:** Up to 200 attendees

## Speaker Benefits

-   ✅ Breakfast and lunch both days
-   ⚠️ Travel/accommodation not specified — likely limited or not covered (typical for DevOpsDays)

## Conference Profile

-   **Focus:** DevOps culture, processes, and tools — with special interest in **AI in DevOps** this year
-   **Size/vibe:** Regional DevOpsDays event, ~200 attendees, single-track + open spaces
-   **Format:** Single-track presentations, ignite talks, open spaces (hallway track style)
-   **Audience:** Development, operations, security, networking, and management professionals

## What They're Looking For

> "We're interested in how you're using AI in DevOps. Tell us how you found novel or interesting ways to effectively improve your DevOps practices using the various AI tools. Be sure to connect the dots between the actions your implementation takes and the underlying principles of DevOps."

**Key insight:** They explicitly want **AI + DevOps** content this year!

## Submission Recommendations

### 🏆 STRONG RECOMMENDATIONS

#### 1. The Copilot Gap: What You're Missing With AI Coding Assistants

**Why it fits well:**

-   Directly addresses AI tools in development workflow
-   Can frame around DevOps principles (automation, continuous improvement)
-   Practical content on effective AI usage

**💡 Angle suggestion:** Position the talk around how Copilot fits into DevOps practices — automating repetitive coding, improving delivery speed, but needing proper context to be effective.

---

#### 2. Testing Your Tests: Mutation Testing in C# with Stryker

**Why it fits well:**

-   Testing/quality is core to DevOps
-   Connects to CI/CD quality gates
-   Practical tool that improves delivery confidence

**💡 Angle suggestion:** Frame around "shifting left" and improving test quality in CI/CD pipelines.

---

#### 3. No More SQLite - How to Write Tests With EF Core Using TestContainers

**Why it fits well:**

-   Containers are core DevOps technology
-   Integration testing in CI/CD pipelines
-   Practical, immediately applicable

**💡 Angle suggestion:** Emphasize the DevOps angle — how TestContainers enables reliable integration tests in CI/CD environments.

---

### ⭐ GOOD OPTIONS

#### Contract Testing with Pact

**Why it fits:** Microservices, CI/CD, testing between services — very DevOps relevant.

#### Manage Cloud Infrastructure With C# Using Pulumi

**Why it fits:** IaC is core DevOps. Could position as alternative to traditional IaC tools.

#### How to Measure Developer Productivity

**Why it fits:** DevOps culture and measurement (DORA metrics are DevOps staples).

---

### ⚠️ TALKS THAT MAY NOT FIT

#### Getting Started With AI For .NET Devs With Agent Framework

**Challenge:** While AI-focused, it's more about building AI apps than using AI for DevOps. Could be a stretch unless you frame it around DevOps use cases.

---

### 💡 SUBMISSION STRATEGY

**Key differentiator:** They want AI + DevOps intersection. Consider:

1. **Reframe Copilot Gap** for DevOps audience — how AI coding assistants fit into DevOps workflows
2. **Submit testing talks** with DevOps/CI-CD framing
3. **Consider an ignite talk** (short format) if you have a punchy 5-minute idea

**Format note:** DevOpsDays uses open spaces heavily — even if your talk isn't selected, attending and participating in open spaces is valuable for networking and learning.
