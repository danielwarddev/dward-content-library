# Orlando Code Camp 2026

## Conference Details

-   **Event:** Orlando Code Camp 2026
-   **Date:** April 11, 2026 (single day)
-   **Location:** Seminole State College, Sanford, Florida
-   **Website:** https://orlandocodecamp.com/
-   **CFP Link:** https://sessionize.com/orlando-code-camp-2026
-   **CFP Deadline:** March 15, 2026 (rolling approvals)
-   **Current submissions:** 124 talks already submitted

## Speaker Benefits

-   ❌ No travel expenses covered
-   ❌ No accommodation covered
-   ✅ Free attendance (event is free for everyone)
-   ✅ Speaker dinner the night before
-   ✅ Post-event party

## Conference Profile

-   **Focus:** Grassroots community code camp — open to all topics and experience levels
-   **Size/vibe:** Community-driven, welcoming to first-time speakers, Florida regional event
-   **Talk format:** 50-minute sessions with 10-minute breaks
-   **Submission limit:** Multiple submissions allowed, but typically only 1 approved per speaker

## What They're Looking For

> "We welcome speakers of all experience levels and technology backgrounds, and we encourage first-time speakers."

> "Speakers are encouraged to submit talks on topics they're passionate about or experience on a daily basis. Blatant product/company pitches are frowned upon."

**Key notes:**

-   Rolling approvals — they approve sessions as submitted to help out-of-town speakers with travel planning
-   Sessions may be shuffled later to balance the schedule
-   All submitted sessions are publicly listed on the CFP page
