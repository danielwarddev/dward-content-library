# AI for Developers Conference 2026

## Conference Details

-   **Name:** AI for Developers 2026
-   **Dates:** December 17-18, 2026
-   **Format:** Online (fully remote)
-   **Timezone:** GMT (Greenwich Mean Time)
-   **CFP Link:** https://aiconference.dev/cfp
-   **Website:** https://aiconference.dev
-   **Capacity:** Limited to 1,000 attendees (first edition)
-   **Session Formats:**
    -   50-minute talks/live-coding sessions (Day 1: December 17)
    -   Full-day hands-on workshops for up to 50 people (Day 2: December 18)
-   **Workshop Compensation:** Paid at your daily rate

## Conference Focus

**"The conference for software developers ready to thrive in the age of AI."**

This is the **1st edition** of this conference. Their focus is empowering professional developers (3+ years experience) building production-grade enterprise software to work effectively with AI, LLMs, and coding agents.

Key tagline: "LLMs and coding agents think, code, test, and ship alongside you. **Not instead of you**."

## Target Audience

-   Professional software developers (3+ years experience)
-   Enterprise software developers
-   Developers building production-grade applications
-   Anyone wanting to thrive with AI-assisted development

## What They're Looking For (From CFP)

The CFP is very specific about what they want:

> "A standout session combines **high energy**, **maximum at-work practicality**, and a **100% signal-to-noise ratio**. We expect you to deliver all three."

### Key Requirements:

1. **High energy** - Interactive, engaging presentation style
2. **Maximum at-work practicality** - Immediately applicable to real work
3. **100% signal-to-noise ratio** - No fluff, all substance
4. **Enterprise focus** - Production-grade, not hobbyist/experimental

## Submission Requirements

The CFP asks for:

-   Full name
-   Email
-   LinkedIn profile
-   Your timezone
-   Previous speaking appearances (video link)
-   Proposed session(s) with:
    -   Title
    -   Brief description (3-5 sentences)
    -   For workshops: Daily rate in USD

**Response time:** Within 10 working days
