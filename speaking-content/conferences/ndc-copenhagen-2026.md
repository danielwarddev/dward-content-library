# NDC Copenhagen 2026

## Conference Details

- **Event:** NDC Copenhagen 2026
- **Dates:** June 1-4, 2026
- **Location:** Copenhagen, Denmark
- **Website:** https://ndccopenhagen.com
- **CFP Link:** https://sessionize.com/ndc-copenhagen-2026
- **CFP Deadline:** February 1, 2026 ⚠️ (18 days left!)

## Speaker Benefits

- ✅ Travel expenses covered
- ✅ Accommodation covered
- ✅ Free conference pass
- ⚠️ No travel/accommodation for lightning talks, co-speakers, or local speakers

## Conference Profile

- **Focus:** Software development across all topics — very open CFP
- **Size/vibe:** Mid-size NDC event, strong .NET/software craftsmanship community
- **Talk formats:** Standard sessions, lightning talks, workshops

## What They're Looking For

From the CFP:

> "NDC Copenhagen 2026 is open to all topics and subjects that interest software developers. If you have something to say, then speak up!"

**Submission guidance:**

- Limit submissions to 3-4 talks (your best ones, not your entire portfolio)
- Include links to videos, slides, or blog posts if you have speaking experience
