# Scenic City Summit 2026

## Conference Details

-   **Name:** Scenic City Summit 2026
-   **Date:** June 5, 2026
-   **Location:** Chattanooga Convention Center, Downtown Chattanooga, TN
-   **Organizer:** Chattanooga Technology Council (ChaTech)
-   **CFP Deadline:** March 15, 2026 (5:30 PM EDT)
-   **CFP Link:** https://sessionize.com/scenic-city-summit-2026/
-   **Website:** https://sceniccitysummit.com
-   **Session Format:** 45-minute sessions
-   **Speaker Benefits:** Free event fee; travel accommodation (1 night) for speakers 50+ miles away

## Conference Focus

Scenic City Summit is the **Southeast's premier technical conference** (celebrating 10+ years!). It brings together developers, automation specialists, and IT professionals of all skill levels. The conference has a strong Microsoft/PowerShell heritage but covers a broad range of topics.

## Target Audience

-   Developers (all levels)
-   Automation specialists
-   IT professionals
-   PowerShell users
-   Microsoft MVPs and community leaders
-   Tech professionals in the Southeast US

## Session Categories (2026 - NEW STRUCTURE)

The 2026 conference has reorganized into **4 thematic tracks**:

### 1. Creating - Building, designing, and innovating

For those who craft solutions, write code, design experiences, and bring ideas to life. Covers:

-   Software development
-   System architecture
-   Product design
-   Prototyping
-   Maker culture
-   Creative process behind technology

### 2. Stewarding - Operating, securing, and sustaining systems

For those who keep technology running, secure, and reliable. Covers:

-   Infrastructure management
-   DevOps practices
-   Security operations
-   Site reliability engineering
-   Compliance
-   Building resilient systems at scale

### 3. Leading - Guiding teams, shaping strategy, and driving change

For those who lead people and influence direction. Covers:

-   Engineering leadership
-   Team dynamics
-   Organizational transformation
-   Product strategy
-   Decision-making frameworks
-   Building healthy tech cultures

### 4. Exploring - Growing skills, sharing knowledge, and evolving practices

For those passionate about continuous learning. Covers:

-   Mentorship
-   Skill development
-   Career growth
-   Learning methodologies
-   Knowledge sharing
