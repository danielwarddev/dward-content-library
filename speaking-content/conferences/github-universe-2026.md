## Audience (from 2025 stats)

- 3,400 attendees, 200+ speakers, 115 sessions
- 45% Individual Contributors (28% Senior IC + 17% IC)
- 33% management (Manager 7% / Sr Manager 8% / Director 11% / Sr Director 7%)
- 8% VP+
- Companies in past attendance: American Airlines, Mercedes-Benz, Spotify, Adobe, Shopify, Intel

## Audience Segments (per submission guide)

- **Builders:** Engineers, Developers, Maintainers, Founders
- **Drivers:** Eng. leads, DevOps leads, Platform leads, Security teams, Founders
- **Leaders:** CISOs, CTOs, CIOs, CxOs, Founders

## Session Levels

- **100 – Introductory:** foundational concepts and mental models
- **200 – Intermediate:** real workflows, tradeoffs, day-to-day lessons
- **300 – Advanced:** scale, failure modes, deep practitioner content

## 2026 Content Tracks

### 1. Build faster, stay in flow (Builders)
- Shortening feedback loops across the SDLC
- CI/CD that developers trust
- AI in the loop, with guardrails
- Onboarding, learning, and developer growth

### 2. Secure every commit (Drivers)
- Security for AI-powered development and AI systems
- Identity, access, and trust at scale
- Designing guardrails, not gates
- Supply chain integrity from source to production

### 3. Automate and scale with confidence (Drivers + Leaders)
- Automation that earns trust
- Scaling CI/CD and delivery systems
- Measuring what matters
- AI adoption at organizational scale

## What They're Looking For

> "We're especially interested in talks that surface tradeoffs, lessons learned, and concrete changes teams made (along with what worked, what didn't, and why)."

Key submission cues from the guide:
- Title = email subject line: short, punchy, persuasive
- Educational over salesy — kick the sales pitch
- Creative formats welcome (storytelling, live demo, interactive exercise)
- Optional 5-min demo/sample video strongly encouraged
- Bio formula: name + descriptor + credibility + role(s) + accolades + a humanizing personal note
