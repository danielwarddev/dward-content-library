# KCDC 2026 (Kansas City Developer Conference)

## Conference Details

- **Event:** KCDC 2026 (18th annual)
- **Dates:** September 9-11, 2026 (Wednesday workshops, Thursday-Friday sessions)
- **Location:** Kansas City Convention Center, Kansas City, MO
- **Website:** https://kcdc.info
- **CFP Link:** https://sessionize.com/kcdc-2026
- **CFP Deadline:** March 9, 2026

## Speaker Benefits

- ✅ Free conference pass
- ✅ 2 nights hotel accommodation
- ✅ Up to $500 domestic travel reimbursement
- ✅ Speaker dinner
- ⚠️ DevRel/developer advocates excluded from travel benefits
- ⚠️ No details on international travel

## Conference Profile

- **Focus:** Broad multi-track developer conference with strong .NET heritage, growing toward 2000+ attendees
- **Size/vibe:** Large regional conference, community-driven, 15 tracks running simultaneously
- **Talk formats:** 1-hour breakout sessions, 4-hour half-day workshops, 8-hour full-day workshops

## What They're Looking For

> "Submit more than one talk. Submitting a single talk greatly reduces your chance of being selected."

**Submission guidance:**

- Be a specialist, not a generalist — unique perspective and knowledge matters
- They receive hundreds of submissions per topic; niche angles stand out
- Include links to past recordings if available
- Workshop speakers get separate benefits (free pass + hotel)

## 2025 Program Analysis (Selected Sessions from Thursday)

### Topic Patterns Observed

**AI/ML was a dominant theme across multiple tracks:**

- Building AI-Powered APIs with ASP.NET Core and Semantic Kernel
- AI Coding Assistants: Lessons from the Trenches
- Unlocking .NET Aspire AI Capabilities
- The Pragmatic AI Toolkit Using C# and .NET
- Beyond the IDE: Building AI Tools with the GitHub Copilot SDK
- AI-Powered Kubernetes Troubleshooting with MCP
- Practical AI Application Development

**Testing was well-represented:**

- Smart Testing: AI-Powered Strategies for Modern Developers
- Simplify Integration Testing with TestContainers
- Pact-ical Guide to Contract Testing: Examples for APIs, GraphQL, and Messaging
- Playwright: Introduction to E2E testing
- Test Smarter, Not Harder with Advanced xUnit

**Cloud/Infrastructure:**

- Kubernetes was a recurring topic (troubleshooting, Helm charts, K8s patterns)
- Azure-focused sessions (Azure cloud services, Azure Functions)
- Platform engineering and Internal Developer Portals

**Soft skills/culture had strong representation:**

- Burnout talks, team dynamics, communication, DEI
- Career development and mentoring sessions

**Architecture pattern talks were popular:**

- Microservices, CQRS, event-driven, clean architecture
- Domain-Driven Design sessions

### Abstract Style Observations

- Titles tend to be descriptive with a hook (colons and subtitles common)
- Practical, demo-heavy talks dominate (not just theory)
- Mix of beginner-friendly and intermediate content
- .NET/C# was the most common technology stack

### 💡 SUBMISSION STRATEGY

KCDC says **submit multiple talks** — single submissions have "greatly reduced" chances. With 15 tracks, you have lots of room. I'd recommend **5-7 submissions** mixing your best across different tracks:

1. **The Copilot Gap** → AI Workflows and Tooling
2. **TestContainers / EF Core** → Testing and QA
3. **AI + Agent Framework for .NET** → AI and Machine Learning
4. **Stryker / Mutation Testing** → Testing and QA
5. **Measure Developer Productivity** → Methodologies and Process Management
6. **Pulumi with C#** → Cloud or DevOps
7. **Hearing and Being Heard** → Human Skills

This spreads across 5-6 tracks and shows range without competing against yourself in the same track too heavily.
