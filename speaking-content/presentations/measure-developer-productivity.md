# How to Measure Developer Productivity

## Description

The effort to measure developer productivity has become increasingly prevalent, and it understandably remains a passionate debate for many. Despite tools like story points and DORA metrics becoming more common, teams and organizations often expend effort measuring these things without knowing why, or what to do with the data once they have it.

Should we be measuring developer productivity at all, if it's even possible? What metrics should be looked at? Are developers being measured doomed to be under close scrutiny?

Having seen both sides of leadership and development, I'll offer insights into the above questions and more. The presentation will cover if it's feasible - or even advisable - to measure developer productivity, which metrics are truly meaningful for your organization, and how to measure them. Properly implemented, these can empower teams to solve problems more effectively and foster a culture of continuous improvement.

Targeted at both both developers and organizational leaders, any person who might have an investment in or be affected by measuring developer productivity will benefit from this session.
